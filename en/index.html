ABOUT NEOCRYSTAL

Rethinking the Possibilities of PMMA.

NeoCrystal provides high-performance PMMA material solutions featuring high transparency, surface hardness and lightweight characteristics.

Our solutions are designed for architecture, interior design, bathrooms and a wide range of applications where transparent materials are required.

Beyond simply supplying transparent plastic, we explore new possibilities that can complement the limitations of conventional materials in terms of weight, performance and processability.

Material Beyond Transparency

NeoCrystal goes beyond transparency. High optical clarity, surface hardness, lightweight performance and versatile processing possibilities open new opportunities for architectural and interior applications.
