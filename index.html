01 / ABOUT

A new standard
for transparent materials.

NeoCrystal focuses on advanced PMMA solutions that combine transparency, surface hardness, lightweight construction and practical performance.

Transparency is only
the beginning.

Conventional transparent materials have long been limited by weight, impact safety, surface durability and processing requirements.

NeoCrystal approaches PMMA from a different perspective — combining optical clarity with practical performance for demanding applications.

From architectural interiors to premium bathroom environments, we aim to provide materials that are lighter, safer and easier to apply.
